About Aspen Montessori

At Aspen Montessori School, we believe every child is naturally curious and capable. Our teachers guide children to explore at their own pace in a prepared, child-centered environment rooted in Maria Montessori's philosophy.

🌱
Child-Led Learning

Children choose their work freely, building independence, focus, and intrinsic motivation from an early age.

🤝
Caring Community

Small class sizes and experienced educators mean every child feels seen, safe, and supported every day.

🎨
Hands-On Materials

Authentic Montessori materials engage all the senses and lay concrete foundations for math, language, and science.
